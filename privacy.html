Privacy & local storage

Public preview · last updated 8 October 2026. This notice describes the bundled FK Financial Kinetics static application, not any future account or analytics service.

What is stored

The app uses your browser’s localStorage under the key fk-casino-v1 to retain your virtual chip balance, play statistics, game results, property progress, and associated simulation records. The older key name is deliberately retained to preserve progress from previous versions. The data is stored in your browser on this device and can be edited or erased by you or your browser.

What is sent

The bundled site contains no user registration, advertising trackers, analytics scripts, payment system, or application backend. The application code does not transmit saved gameplay records to FK servers. When you visit a hosted website, the hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP addresses and access logs under its own policies. The operator must confirm the actual host and update this notice before public publication.

Control your data

Use the game’s Reset control to restart the local simulation, or clear this website’s browser storage to remove the stored save. Reset does not delete any hosting-provider access logs. Browser storage is device-specific and may be lost if you clear site data or use private browsing.
