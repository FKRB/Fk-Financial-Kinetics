About FK Financial Kinetics

Capital in Motion. FK is an interactive educational project connecting probability simulations with treasury, valuation, credit analysis, capital allocation and financial risk.

Simulation, not financial advice

All games use virtual chips only. There are no deposits, withdrawals, cash prizes or exchangeable rewards. Banking, market, investment and corporate examples are simplified hypothetical models; they are not offers, live market quotations, underwriting recommendations, investment advice, or substitutes for professional analysis.

Understand the limitations

  • Casino probabilities can be defined by game rules, while market probabilities are uncertain and estimated.
  • Results from small samples can differ substantially from expected values.
  • Deal, treasury, credit and project models use illustrative assumptions and omit many real-world factors such as fees, covenants, taxes, timing, regulations and transaction-specific details.
  • Financial analytics based on virtual chips do not represent a real company’s revenue, profits or cash flows.
  • Monte Carlo results are model-dependent, not forecasts or guarantees.
