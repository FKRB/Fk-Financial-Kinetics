European roulette EV

Even-money red bet: 18 winning pockets and 19 losing pockets across 37 equally likely outcomes.

Player EV = (18/37 × stake) + (19/37 × −stake). The casino's theoretical expected profit is the opposite sign. This is a known mathematical probability model.

Why the distinction matters

Roulette probabilities are fixed by the wheel model. Investment scenario probabilities and returns are estimates that can be wrong, change through time, and omit outcomes. Expected return is therefore a model output—not a promise or a complete measure of risk.
